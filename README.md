# DataPulse - Business Insights & Data Visualization Platform

## Key Features

* KPI tracking: Revenue, Profit, Margin, Orders, and Customers
* Monthly sales and profit trends
* Product and regional performance analysis
* Top products by revenue
* High-revenue, low-margin product analysis
* Interactive filters for year, quarter, category, customer segment, region, and return status

## Business Questions

* What are the total revenue, profit, and profit margin?
* How do revenue and profit change over time?
* Which categories and regions perform best in terms of revenue and profit?
* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which high-revenue products have low profit margins?

## Data Quality

* Invalid order dates excluded from the main analysis
* Negative quantities retained in net calculations
* Missing customer and product IDs handled
* Return status treated as an indicator, not confirmed returns

## Future Improvements

* Power BI dashboard
* Advanced customer segmentation
* Sales forecasting
* Additional business performance metrics
